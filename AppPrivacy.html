QWally Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Quantrex Apps Projects ("we," "us," or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and what rights you may have with respect to your information when you use QWally or related services.

By using QWally, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app or services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, secure, and support the app experience. Depending on how you use QWally, we may collect:

Personal information you provide

  • Account details such as name, email address, username, and profile information when you register or update your account.
  • Customer support messages and other communication details you send to us.
  • User-generated content you upload, store, or transmit through the app.

Information collected automatically

  • Device information such as model, operating system version, app version, device identifiers, and language settings.
  • Usage data such as feature usage, interactions, session duration, crash reports, errors, app performance data, and app diagnostics.
  • Log data such as IP address, access timestamps, connectivity information, and referral data.

Location information

  • We may collect coarse or precise location data only when you enable location permissions for a feature that requires it.
  • Location data may be used for features such as location-based recommendations, region filters, or service personalization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and improve the QWally app and its features.
  • To authenticate accounts and maintain secure access to the service.
  • To personalize content and remember preferences.
  • To analyze performance, diagnose bugs, fix issues, and improve stability.
  • To respond to support requests and provide customer care.
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other harmful activity.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only in limited circumstances, including:

  • Trusted service providers that support hosting, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, or app functionality.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or compliance obligations.
  • Business transfers in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of substantially all assets.

Where required, we will use contracts and safeguards designed to limit how third-party providers use your information.

4. Your Choices and Rights

You may have rights under applicable law, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account or personal data, where lawful.
  • Withdraw consent for optional permissions such as location, camera, notifications, or analytics where applicable.
  • Opt out of marketing communications if such communications are offered.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in the Contact section below.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children’s Privacy

QWally is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or authorization.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without valid consent, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, product functionality, or business practices. The updated version will be posted with a revised "Last Updated" date.
